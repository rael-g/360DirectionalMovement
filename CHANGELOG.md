# Changelog

## 0.2.0

### Added

- F11 switches the mod off and back on without leaving the game

### Changed

- The body is turned only while walking, jogging and jumping with a weapon put
  away. Sprinting, ladders, zero g, sitting and having a weapon out are left to
  the game.

### Fixed

- Ladders are climbable again, and no longer trap the character between decks
- Zero g no longer spins the character on the spot
- Drawing a weapon no longer leaves the body aiming away from the camera

## 0.1.1

### Fixed

- Sitting no longer leaves the character crooked, most visibly in a cockpit
- The body is no longer rotated in first person

## 0.1.0

First release.
